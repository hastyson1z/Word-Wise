How to Play 🪢

Rules

  1. Pick a difficulty, timer, and word source, then press Start Game.
  2. A hidden word from a category (e.g. Animals, Space) appears as blank letter boxes.
  3. Guess letters using the on-screen keyboard or your physical keyboard (A–Z).
  4. Correct letters fill in every matching blank and turn the key green.
  5. Wrong letters cost one life, turn the key red, and add a body part to the gallows.
  6. Stuck? Tap Hint to reveal a clue — but it costs one life, so use it wisely.
  7. Guess every letter before your lives (or the timer) run out to win the round.
  8. Use Restart to retry the same word, or New Game to set up fresh options.

What You Gain by Winning 🎓

  • Vocabulary growth — each round introduces a new word across 8 knowledge areas, from programming terms to world geography.
  • Spelling accuracy — reconstructing a word letter-by-letter reinforces correct spelling far more than passively reading it.
  • General knowledge — categories like Countries, Space, and Technology expose you to facts you can carry into schoolwork or conversation.
  • Deductive reasoning — working out likely letters from context and word length exercises logical thinking, the same skill used in problem-solving.
  • Working memory — tracking guessed letters, remaining lives, and the emerging word pattern trains short-term memory and focus.
  • Resilience under pressure — timer and hard-mode play build quick decision-making and composure when options narrow.
  • Curiosity — a random fun fact after every win turns each victory into a small, memorable piece of trivia.
